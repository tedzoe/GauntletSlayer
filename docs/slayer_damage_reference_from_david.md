# Slayer Edition damage reference (provided by David, Oct 25 2026)
Source: an online search summary David pasted. Values are approximate community estimates, relative to base monster HP: small common ~30, strong ~48, casters ~96.
Base hero HP: Warrior 100, Valkyrie 75, Elf 50, Wizard 50, Necromancer 50.

## Warrior (Thor)
- Slash combo, 7 hits: weak 14 (Stagger Small); hits 4 and 7 are strong 42 (Stagger Large). Full combo 154 (~46 DPS).
- Smash: 50 (~60 DPS), Stagger Large, Lethal, Brutal.
- Rush (dash): 8 dmg, invulnerable while charging, knocks down small enemies.
  - Super Slash (Rush+attack): 4-hit 42,14,14,42.
  - Leap Attack (Rush+smash): 50, Lethal to small and casters.
- Weapons: Thor's Axe: Destructo Spin, 8x20 = 160, 2.5s invuln. Herald of the War God: Call to War, 8 AoE taunt; Axe Throw, 28/axe (~62 DPS). Murder: Demonic Cleave, 140 forward arc, invuln. Deathpunch: Turbo-Slam, 140 + 14 AoE.
- Talismans: Improvise grenade 210. Manifest Hunger turkeys (5 enemies, 10s, +20 HP). The Prize: giant 12s, invuln, ~+65% dmg.

## Elf (Questor)
- Quick Shot: 4 arrows/s x 14 (~56 DPS); 8 vs spawners.
- Sniper Shot: 36 (~47 DPS), unlimited range, Lethal; 18 vs spawners.
- Circle Tumble (dash): no dmg, passes through enemies. Fires and runs at full speed with no slowdown.
- Weapons: Questor's Bow: Magic Bomb / Explosive Shot 56 (+2 direct), 2s fuse. Deep Woods: Root Field (8s hold) / Root Shot 22 first, 14 after, pierce. Shadowstrike: Smoke Bomb 14 + 4s invis / Shadow Shot 202 + 14 splash. The Messenger: Vault Shot 56 + 44 burn/8s / Mortar Shot 154 + 6x14 clusters + 44 burn.
- Talismans: Ultimate Arrow 438 piercing. Spirit Guardians 3 wisps 12s, 14/tick (~42 DPS). Dryad's Song: party stealth 10s + heal 8.

## Valkyrie (Thyra)
- Slash combo, 7 hits: 8 each; hits 4 and 7 are 42. Full 124 (~47 DPS).
- Spear Thrust (dash): 42, Stagger Large, Lethal.
- Shield Block: blocks all directional attacks, kills small entities (ghosts, skeletons, baby spiders) on contact, reflects fireballs (>80). NO cooldown, hold indefinitely (David's rule).
- Weapons: Wargear: Shield Throw 56 ricochet, x2 vs spawners (112). Freedom Protector: Intercept 14 / Spear Throw 28 (~76 DPS). Final Justice: Extreme Prejudice 70 spin, invuln. Wrath of the Heavens: Smite 188 + 12 splash; combo finisher 3 chain arcs x14.
- Talismans: Righteous Retribution 280 arc. Divine Aegis: party trap immunity 6s. Summon Valkyries: 3 for 12s, thrust 68 / strike 12.

## Wizard (Merlin)
- Firebolts 1.8/s x 22 (~40 DPS). Fireball mortar 56 (~28 DPS), knockdown small. Flaming Serpents (dash/teleport): 16 + 96 burn/12s.
- Ice: Ice Beam ~56 DPS + Slow 3; Freezing Wave freeze 3s; Cold Sphere 9s slow zone.
- Lightning: Chain Lightning 22/jump; Shock Blast 14 knockdown; Storm Barrier 6s.
- Demon (Sinister Sentience): Hellfire ~40 DPS; Searing Blast 70 + 44 burn; Burning Orb 6x12.
- Channeling (Endrich's Sphere): Rift Jump up to 96; Repeater Bolts 6.5/s x 8 (~52 DPS); Storm Barrage 4 orbs x (42+14).
- Void (Hollow Chapters): Dark Torrent 4 to 30+ DPS; Vacuum pull + slow 5s; Invisibility 6s.
- Talismans: Arcane Blast 106. Polymorph 6 homing orbs. Barrier wall 20s ~1200 HP.

## Necromancer (Lilith)
- Skeleton Slash 2.5/s x 8 (~20 DPS). Skeleton Smash coffin 46 + 2 splash, draws aggro. Ghostwalk dash 4 + Slow 5, harvests souls.
- Soul Power up to 5 tiers from finishing blows.
- Sceptre: skeleton soldiers 15s, 8 dmg/30 HP; charged 12/48. Eternal Night: shades 28 each, max 20 = 560; Cold Bolt 36 (~76 DPS). Necrotic Vessel: archers 20s, 14/arrow; charged flame 22. Vampire King: Domination charm 3 to 15s, x2 dmg taken, vampiric +1 HP per bar.
- Talisman: Dark Hunger 6s, 8 DPS aura + 2 HP/s per enemy.
