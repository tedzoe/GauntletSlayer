# Slayer Edition gameplay video notes (from David's clips, /workspace/video/slayer_part0-3.mp4)

Footage shows only the WIZARD in play (Warrior appears only in lobbies). No Valkyrie, Elf or Necromancer footage.

## Wizard moves
- Fire Stream (~2:16, ~2:59): channeled narrow dense cone, medium length; rapid continuous damage, brief burn, slight stagger on small foes; no wind-up/cooldown.
- Explosive Fireball (~2:38, ~3:00): wind-up + staff swing; bright medium-speed orb; explodes into circle ~3-4x hero width; high dmg, knockback, one-shots small foes; brief fire residue; recast every ~1-2 s.
- Void bolt (~3:47, ~4:08): quick staff flick; small fast purple sphere; tiny impact; slight knockback; near semi-auto rate.
- Void Vortex / black hole (~3:52, ~5:35): staff thrust up, placed at target; lingering purple swirl ~4x hero width; continuous damage for ~4-5 s; has cooldown.
- Lightning/energy beam (~5:06): braced pointing; wide straight instant beam across screen; pierces all in line; channeled ~1-2 s.
- Freezing burst (~4:10, ~5:19): staff raised; white/cyan circular blast; freezes foes solid (white, immobile) a few seconds.
- Repelling shockwave (~5:14, unsure if spell or relic): large fast expanding cyan ring around hero; knocks all nearby foes far back.
- HUD: three element slots (fire red, lightning/ice blue-yellow, void purple) bottom-center, two larger spell squares above them.

## Enemies / hits-to-kill
- Risen Mummy: slow melee swarmers, walk straight at hero. 1 fireball, 1-2 void bolts, ~1 s of fire stream.
- Monstrous/Bloated Mummy: bigger, same speed, tanky. 2-3 fireballs or ~3 s fire stream.
- Corrupted (green) Mummy: noticeably faster; dies in green explosive burst; 1 fireball.
- No ranged enemies shown; ranged damage came from floor arrow traps (~3:39, ~4:18).

## HUD / pickups
- Bottom-left: red HP bar, portrait/name, potion count (blue bottle icon), key count (gold key icon). Bottom-center: abilities. Top-right: Skull Coins.
- Food: meat on plates, auto pickup, heals a chunk, floating green "+ Health".
- Potions: blue glowing bottles, auto pickup, increment the counter.
- Death: HP empty -> a few seconds later a golden beam revives hero at full HP, costing one Skull Coin.
