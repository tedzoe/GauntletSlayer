# Warrior (Thor) mechanics reference, applies to Sir Theo
Source: Lein's Steam guide, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1789707871 (David, Oct 25 2026)
- A slow but powerful melee fighter with high HP and many invincibility frames; has to stay mobile because he has no shield.
- Slash Attack: slowish startup. Stuns (interrupts) LIGHT enemies only and interrupts casters. Seven-hit sequence: hit 4 is the white-trail Super Slash, which stuns heavies; hit 7 is a 360-degree circle attack with long recovery afterward.
- Smash Attack: slow, powerful, best against mini-bosses and spawners. Destroys projectiles. Stuns heavies. The stun is instant, before the damage lands, and wider than the hit.
- Rush (dash): faster than running, invincible for the whole dash, kills the weakest enemies on contact (skeletons, ghosts). Can't pass through heavies. Short startup. Can't steer once started. If it runs to the end, it flows straight into running.
  - Super Slash (slash during Rush) is the same move as hit 4. It stuns light and heavy enemies, the sequence continues from hit 5, and it stops the Rush early with a brief root.
  - Leap Attack (smash during Rush): high damage, jumps OVER enemies including heavies, extends the invincibility, stuns heavies.
  - Holding Rush while using a potion skips the More of Thor transform's immobility.
- Demonic Cleave (Murder): about a 6s cooldown. A strong forward attack whose sides reach almost all the way around him except mid-back, with a tiny stun ring on every side. He's invincible and stays in place (no slide). One-shots egg sacs.
- Destructo Spin (default axe): about a 10s cooldown, similar kill power, but he SLIDES while it runs.
- More of Thor: giant, invincible from the start of the transform, more damage; stuck in place during the transform. Death still kills him.
- Ring of Mirrors (Thor variant): illusions that block one side and pull aggro.
