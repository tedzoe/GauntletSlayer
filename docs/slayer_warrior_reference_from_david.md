# Warrior (Thor) mechanics reference, applies to Sir Theo
Source: Lein's Steam guide, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1789707871 (David, Oct 25 2026)
- A slow but powerful melee fighter with high HP and many invincibility frames; has to stay mobile because he has no shield.
- Slash Attack: slowish startup. Stuns (interrupts) LIGHT enemies only and interrupts casters. Seven-hit sequence: hit 4 is the white-trail Super Slash, which stuns heavies; hit 7 is a 360-degree circle attack with long recovery afterward.
- Smash Attack: slow, powerful, best against mini-bosses and spawners. Destroys projectiles. Stuns heavies. The stun is instant, before the damage lands, and wider than the hit.
- Rush (dash): faster than running, invincible for the whole dash, kills the weakest enemies on contact (skeletons, ghosts). Can't pass through heavies. Short startup. Can't steer once started. If it runs to the end, it flows straight into running.
  - Super Slash (slash during Rush) is the same move as hit 4. It stuns light and heavy enemies, the sequence continues from hit 5, and it stops the Rush early with a brief root.
  - Leap Attack (smash during Rush): high damage, jumps OVER enemies including heavies, extends the invincibility, stuns heavies.
  - Holding Rush while using a potion skips the More of Thor transform's immobility.
- Demonic Cleave (Murder): about a 6s cooldown. A strong forward attack whose sides reach almost all the way around him except mid-back, with a tiny stun ring on every side. He's invincible and stays in place (no slide). One-shots egg sacs.
- Destructo Spin (default axe): about a 10s cooldown, similar kill power, but he SLIDES while it runs.
- More of Thor: giant, invincible from the start of the transform, more damage; stuck in place during the transform. Death still kills him.
- Ring of Mirrors (Thor variant): illusions that block one side and pull aggro.

### 2. anim_22.gif: Smash Attack

### 4. anim_20.gif: Super Slash (Slash during Rush)
- **Section:** Attacks. Floats right, before "It's essentially an upgraded Slash Attack...".
- **What it shows:** Thor comes in on a Rush from the right (about 0.25-0.5) with the axe already swinging. - **For Theo:** Slash pressed during Rush gives an instant big white crescent. It stuns light AND heavy enemies, does more damage than Slash, stops the dash on the spot, and roots him briefly. Afterward the Slash chain resumes at hit 5. It's the same attack as hit 4 of the chain and must use the same visual.

### 6. anim_17.gif: Demonic Cleave (Murder weapon ability)
- **Section:** Attacks. Floats right, before "Demonic Cleave is a very powerful attack...". The guide does have a GIF of it; this is it.
- **What it shows:** Idle until about 0.55. At about 0.6 a bright WHITE RADIAL FLASH bursts around his body (the invincibility starting). From about 0.65 to 1.0 a pale glow lightens the whole area around him while the axe goes STRAIGHT UP overhead, with a small STARBURST sparkle at the tip (about 0.78-0.96). At about 1.08 he swings down and around into a deep forward lunge, cape flying out behind. The swing leaves a dense swirling WHITE crescent of several curved streaks wrapping his front and left side (forward-weighted, with the back open). Then a small separate white crescent WISP flies off from the slash toward the lower left and fades (about 1.1-1.5). His feet stay in place the whole time. Crouched recovery runs about 1.5-2.4, then idle. Flash to hit is about 0.5 s. In the full-color GIFs (Oy3rZLF, e03lmoV) the same cast shows his whole body turning SOLID RED inside a red-orange halo, with the starburst on the raised axe and a cream-gold wisp flying off. So the in-game invincibility tint is red. There's no shockwave line and no burning ground in any of them.
- **Guide text:** Attacks section: "Demonic Cleave is a very powerful attack that also grants Thor invincibility for a brief window of time. It is the weapon ability of the weapon, Murder, and therefore operates on a cooldown. Enemies directly surrounding him will be stunned for a very short moment. This buys you just enough time to walk away or use Rush immediately afterwards." Weapons section: "Do not be deceived by Demonic Cleave's relatively modest display in contrast with some of Thor's other weapon abilities." It's considered his best weapon for safety and versatility, thanks to three things plus its short cooldown.
  - Concentrated attack power: "the bulk of the force of the attack is directed forward" but "It covers nearly the entire area surrounding Thor except near the middle of his back." A small, brief "force" stuns anything touching him on all sides, a "safety circle", so "follow through with your next course of action very promptly".
  - Invincibility: he can dodge timed spike traps, stand on pressure-plate spikes, and dodge a Cultist Sorcerer's hex if he times it right.
  - Control: "he stands his ground and remains perfectly in place", and the safety circle gives "just enough time to use Rush without getting hit, even if surrounded by a huge mob."
  - The short cooldown means you pick the exact moment you need the invincibility.
- **Weapon notes, kept and merged:** about a 6 s cooldown. Destructo Spin (default axe) is about 10 s and SLIDES; Cleave does not slide. FAQ damage is 140 (230 with More of Thor). It one-shots egg sacs and hits two summoning stones if they're lined up. Combos: a dashing slash followed by a Cleave does 252, and a Cleave can cancel a combo.
- **For Theo:** a white flash, then a red body tint for the invincibility window, the axe straight up with a tip sparkle, and one big forward-weighted swirl crescent that wraps nearly all the way round, plus a small wisp flying out. A tiny all-around stun ring right at his body. Rooted, no slide, about a 6 s cooldown. Keep the look modest: no screen shake or shockwave on the scale of the other weapon specials.
- **Game mismatch to fix in the animation and damage pass:** data.js describes Hellforged Cleaver/Demonic Cleave as a "1m x 6m shockwave, burning ground, 8s cooldown". The guide says 6 s, a near-360 forward-weighted cleave, no shockwave line, and now confirmed visually by anim_17: no burning ground either. The only projectile-looking piece is the small cosmetic wisp. Treat it as visual only unless the FAQ says otherwise.

## Theo animation checklist (no two moves may look alike)
- **Slash (chain hits 1-3, 5-6):** a short shoulder wind-up of about 0.3 s, a flat sweep that alternates sides, a cream-to-orange-to-red trail, a step forward each hit, and about 0.4 s between hits.
- **Super Slash (chain hit 4 or Slash in Rush):** a thick icy blue-white multi-streak C crescent that stays white about 0.2 s. Out of Rush it stops him dead with a brief root, then the chain resumes at hit 5. Stuns heavies.
- **Circle (chain hit 7):** a one-frame blue-white flash, then a full 360 GOLD ring fading to orange-red, then about 0.6-0.7 s of recovery that Rush can cancel (buffered).
- **Smash:** an overhead raise, a small forward hop with the shadow separating, a vertical slam with a spark or puff at the axe head, and a long planted recovery. Instant stun wider than the hit. Destroys projectiles.
- **Rush:** a quick crouch, a thin red line along the dash path, then a straight low dash with the cape streaming and a red-pink streak behind. Invincible, can't steer, and flows into a run at the end.
- **Leap (Smash in Rush):** a real airborne arc with the shadow apart, landing in an overhead slam with a DUST CLOUD ring. Clears enemies including heavies, stays invincible, stuns heavies.
- **Demonic Cleave:** a white radial flash, then a SOLID RED body tint and halo, the axe straight up with a STARBURST at the tip, then a deep lunge with a forward-weighted swirl of several white streaks wrapping nearly all the way round, plus a small wisp flying off. Feet planted, tiny all-around stun ring, about a 6 s cooldown, deliberately "modest" (no shockwave, no burning ground).
- **More of Thor:** a light beam from above, a white flash, a potion flask icon over his head, a red aura, a gold starburst of rays, and growth to a giant red-tinted Theo. Invincible from the flash, rooted unless he's in a held Rush.
- **Enemy feedback, shared by all moves:** a white hit flash on the target, a stagger on a stun, blood or debris bursts, and summoning stones that explode in a white then orange-red fire burst. Heavies must visibly IGNORE Slash hits 1-3 and 5-6 (no stagger) but stagger from Super Slash, Smash, Leap and Cleave.
