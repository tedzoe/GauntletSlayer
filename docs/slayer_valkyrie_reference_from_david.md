# Valkyrie (Thyra) mechanics reference
Source: Steam guide "Thyra the Valkyrie" by Lein, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1798796700 (David sent it Oct 25 2026). Applies to Dawnbringer Gela.

- Slash sequence, 7 hits: hits 4 and 7 are HEAVY (white blur trail), deal significant damage and stun large enemies. Rhythm is 1-2-3-[4]-1-2-[3]. The stun from a heavy lasts just long enough to reach the next heavy, so repeating the sequence can stun-lock one heavy indefinitely. Each slash steps her slightly forward. Light slashes stun (interrupt) light enemies.
- Spear Thrust (dash): powerful lunge, stuns heavy enemies, instantly slays all lesser foes in its path, and gives invincibility during the thrust (can dodge hexes). Has a startup delay plus an end-lag delay where she can't move or attack. Hitting a wall stuns her briefly. Without canceling it covers about running pace.
  - Shield cancel: holding block during or at the end of the thrust cancels the end lag immediately.
  - Wall cancel: if block is held when the thrust STARTS, she doesn't stun on wall impact and passes THROUGH enemies, mini-bosses included.
  - Both combined: chaining thrust then shield cancel moves faster than running.
- Shield Block: blocks any attack, explosion or AoE from the facing direction (aim at the explosion center); deflects projectiles and fireballs back. When the shield is struck, she bashes and pushes back ALL enemies directly in front of her (0 damage), interrupting casters behind them. Gives a brief follow-up window after each bash. Small enemies (ghosts, skeletons, baby spiders) die when they touch the shield. While blocking, movement and facing are independent (strafe). Held indefinitely with no cooldown. Does not stop slow effects. Doesn't deflect while Divine Aegis is active (projectiles pass through).
- Extreme Prejudice (Final Justice weapon ability): sweeping axe spin around her that stuns surrounding enemies, then hits for damage; invincible for the duration; on a cooldown. Used to link two Spear Thrusts.
- Relics noted: Boots of Ranadam (faster, slightly faster cooldowns, slows nearby enemies); Tempest Locket (tornado that clears light enemies and briefly stuns heavies, light damage to all, breaks cracked walls).
- Talisman: Divine Aegis protects all heroes from almost everything except Death for a period of time and heals slightly.
- Freedom Protector alt weapon: spear thrust becomes a javelin throw, near Elf-sniper strength, twice as fast, short build-up.
